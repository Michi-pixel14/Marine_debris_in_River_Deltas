Automatic pipeline collecting climate time-series (precipitation, discharge) for an Area of Interest in the vicinity of a river delta, automatically detects extreme events with a statistical approach and downloads correponding Sentinel-2 multi-spectral data. Pre- and post-event data are compared both by studying their spectral evolution (computation of the Floating Debris Index) and processed with the pre-trained Marine Debris Detector to analyze the trend of debris in correspondance of such detected extreme events.
